# YouTube build videos

Claude can't open YouTube from the cloud, so `tools/yt_transcript.py` runs on Nick's PC
and saves each video's description, chapters and full transcript to `videos/raw/`.
Claude then reads those and writes the short summaries below.

## How to add videos

On Nick's PC (or ask Claude to do it through Remote Control):

```bash
pip install -U yt-dlp youtube-transcript-api
python tools/yt_transcript.py https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID
python tools/yt_transcript.py --from-md builds/wand-and-spell-shortlist.md   # every link in a file
git add videos && git commit -m "Add video transcripts" && git push
```

Then ask Claude to "summarize the new videos in videos/raw".

## Summaries

Read 2026-11-09 and 2026-11-10 from the transcripts in `raw/`. Captions are YouTube's auto-generated ones, so some item names
were garbled. I only name items I'm confident about. Most videos are **3.29 or older**, so re-check against 3.30 patch notes; the ten added on 2026-11-11 are about 3.30, except Telvanni's 3.29 Conduit update and Tuadh's October practice run.

### Takeaways for Nick

**Added 2026-11-11, late** (seven more pulled on Nick's PC with his go-ahead, all with full transcripts; seven PoB codes saved in `builds/pob/`):
- **Lightning Tendrils Elementalist (Peuget2, 3.30)** is tanky and hits hard, and its author is blunt about the cost: "you need to stand still", and it won't clear off screen. He levels with **Power Siphon** to the second lab and later adds Cast while Channelling.
- **Lightning Conduit Elementalist (Telvanni, 3.29)**: Conduit from the first lab, "zero deaths going through the campaign".
- **Kinetic Bolt of Fragmentation Assassin (Chem)** is a wand build from level 1 but fragile: 30K EHP on the gear that killed Eater and Exarch.
- **Kinetic Blast of Clustering Necromancer (lifewithoutpants, 3.30)**: day-one gear clears a four-Voidstone T16 in under two minutes, with no Kinetic Fusillade step in the levelling.
- **Winter Orb**: Fubgun says it's "probably really not a build worth playing anymore" (endgame "50 to 60% less damage"); lifewithoutpants puts the gem's own loss at "around 30%".

**Added 2026-11-11** (three 3.30 videos pulled on Nick's PC, all with full transcripts):
- **Penance Brand Elementalist is #2 on a post-patch top 10** built from last league's poe.ninja numbers (The Divine Ledger). Winter Orb is dropped from that list: "a real cut, not a tap", but not dead.
- **The same video caught an error in our notes:** the Chieftain, Elementalist and Occultist lines in the patch notes are "under the Ruthless heading. They don't touch normal leagues." Checked against the notes and corrected in the shortlist and nerf watch.
- **Bossing matters this league**: ItFightsBack drops Righteous Fire because "the whole league mechanic is based around bosses".
- **Bladefall on an Assassin** gets a theory-video endorsement (self-cast, not Ky's trigger version).

**Added 2026-11-10** (17 more videos pulled on Nick's PC; 8 have full transcripts, 2 have no commentary, 7 have captions turned off):
- **Bladefall + Blade Blast Assassin (Ky)** is the best-documented early bosser on the list. Spellslinger from level 37, then **cast on crit at ~level 85 for about 3 div**, triggered by Lancing Steel of Spraying on a foil (ranged, one button). Ky had all four Voidstones on day 2 with under 5 div of gear.
- **Penance Brand ignite** pops more than we thought: one cast spreads ignite across the screen, and Obliteration or Lost Unity add explosions. Goratha says the brand works from the first lab; animeprincess swaps at the end of the campaign.
- **Power Siphon cast on crit exists**: Kross's 3.30 "CoC Archmage Firestorm of Pelting triggered by Power Siphon" Hierophant. One small video. Its PoB was read on Nov 10 and doesn't back the "tanky" pitch (46.7K EHP, 14.3K physical max hit at level 97) or the DPS figure; see [the shortlist](../builds/wand-and-spell-shortlist.md#power-siphon-cast-on-crit-what-the-pob-shows).
- **Kinetic Blast of Clustering**: its own guide author says single target is "trash" until Whispers of Infinity and behind Winter Orb after.
- **Kinetic Bolt Elementalist** and **Volatile Dead Spellslinger** videos have no usable commentary. Only their descriptions and chapter lists could be read.

**From 2026-11-09:**

- **Winter Orb Occultist** comes out best for Nick's rules. It's the main skill from about level 28 and stays that way. One creator
  reached all 4 Voidstones on a **5-link** with cheap uniques. It's tanky with CI and capped block, and it auto-targets.
  The main care point is mana: your mana regen has to cover its cost.
- **Kinetic Fusillade**. Self-cast is a few-divine build that gets to T16. One creator says it's squishy and will "100% get randomly
  one-shot" (topside tree, so softcore only). The ballista test hit T11–12 maps 4 hours in with plenty of damage, but died a lot.
  3.29 made KF *easier* early (less attack-speed homework) and a bit weaker at the top end.
- **Kinetic Blast of Clustering** breaks the "no late switch" rule. The videos level it as spell → Firestorm → Power Siphon totems,
  then swap to KBoC at 70–80 and to CI/chaos at 90+. Legi1 calls it B/B- tier and weak early after the 3.29 nerf.
- **Spark Archmage** is still "insanely strong" and very tanky per Big Ducks (75% block / 75% spell block). It's best in corridors
  and weak in open maps. The detailed endgame video is from 3.25 (2024), so its prices are old.
- The 3.30 "DoT Slinger" video is a 4-hour stream with little useful talk. It's a cold-DoT Spellslinger levelling setup (DoT, so less Nick's style).
