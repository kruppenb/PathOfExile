## The league mechanic: Fates and the Wicker Man

- Witch **coven encounters** appear in areas. Beating them lets you pick **Fate cards** from a cauldron.
- You can **invoke up to 3 Fates on an area's boss**. Fated bosses are harder and give better rewards (currency, uniques, maps).
  Later on you get 4–5 Fates per area and choose which 3 to use.
- **Combining matching Fates** gives bonus rewards (rarity, special/Exceptional support gems, unique flasks). A **duplicate Fate roughly triples** the first Fate's reward.
- More than two dozen **new campaign bosses** were reportedly added so every area has a boss *(single source)* — relevant to leveling speed.
- Fated bosses drop **Viscera** (flesh, bone, skin, carapace) that you put on the **Wicker Man**: four slots (head, arm, torso, leg),
  three tiers per boss, top tier endgame only. Bonuses like life, regen, poison *(counts and values single source)*. This is a **character power system** — likely matters for build choice once values are known.
- **Grim Fates** drop from high-level atlas bosses. They change pinnacle bosses (e.g. Sirus storms become crows). Beating a Grim-Fated pinnacle drops Pinnacle Viscera and leads to the final **Coven Queen**.

**Build implications (early read):** a build that kills bosses comfortably gets more from the league mechanic than a pure speed-clear build,
because the rewards sit on harder bosses. Favour starters with real single-target damage and good defences.

## Endgame / systemic changes

### Scarabs removed → Threads of Creation
- Threads drop from normal endgame loot, one type per mechanic: **Abyss, Blight, Legion, Breach, Incursion, Betrayal, Bestiary, Expedition, Ultimatum, Ritual**.
- Put in the Map Device to add/guarantee a mechanic and scale monster rarity/difficulty. More Threads = higher chance of extra powerful effects.
- Up to **three mechanics at once**; the third slot reportedly comes from finishing the Atlas questline.
- Unclear until patch notes: whether it's up to 8 Threads per mechanic or tiers 1–8, and whether Threads are consumed or persist.
- GGG's stated intent: the baseline juice Scarabs gave moves into the Atlas tree; Threads add random jackpots on top.

### Loot rework
- **Fewer items drop.** Magic and rare items drop with **visible tiers (up to 5)**. Higher tiers = better mods, bases, sockets and links.
- Tier 5 magic items can drop **fractured** *(single source)*.
- **Item rarity** now improves the *quality* of drops (chance of currency, div cards, gems, uniques) rather than quantity. Item quantity is no longer a reward lever.
- Divines are meant to come mostly from tough rare and unique monsters.
- **Update your loot filter** (FilterBlade will have a 3.30 version).

### Atlas
- **Atlas passive tree rebuilt from scratch** with new clusters, absorbing old Scarab effects and Thread-linked nodes.
  Named notables: True Terrors, Fate Bespoken, golden orbs from map bosses, Ghostflame Communion, House Supremacy, By the Lanternlight, Servants of the Well.
- **Atlas regions tied to mechanics**; completing maps in a region strengthens its mechanic.
- **Mirage becomes core** (random spawn or map modifier).
- **Harvest:** Sacred Grove removed; tangled roots in maps accept currency for modifiers.
- **Beyond:** killing a boss no longer stops portals.
- **Incursion:** Atziri replaces the Vaal Omnitect in the Temple; corrupted items can be corrupted again *(single source)*.
- Reported tweaks: Blight (mycotic lanes), Ritual (optional waves), Ultimatum (double-down), Expedition (bigger sites), Bestiary (no yellow beasts needed for recipes), Gwennen gambles on your items, Delve/Heist/Sanctum marked on atlas.

## Classes, skills, balance

**Nothing confirmed yet.** No source reports specific 3.30 class, ascendancy or skill changes; GGG said patch notes would include regular balance changes.
ExileCon Q&A (per [Output Lag](https://outputlag.com/news/path-of-exile-3-30-removes-the-15-year-old-rule-that-cut-boss-currency-drops-by-80/), Nov 8): **light character balance only**, no new ascendancy or skill package, bigger balance work aimed at 3.31. Luminary gets **Arcane Link** (added lightning damage from your max mana). New gem **Fireball of Radiation**. The old rule that rares/uniques dropped 80% less currency, scarabs and div cards (magic 50% less) is removed.
One pre-patch article flags that 3.29's **Luminary mercenary / Kinetic mercenary** setups looked overtuned and might be nerfed.
