# 3.30 day-one learnings: Lightning Tendrils, Penance Brand and the top builds

Collected 2026-11-14, about 24 hours after launch, at Nick's request (he was in Act 7 on Lightning Tendrils, Peuget2's route). Sources: a poe.ninja ladder snapshot, two Reddit megathreads plus 23 other threads, creators' day-one videos, and GGG's hotfix notes. Raw material is in [news/raw/](../news/raw/) and [videos/raw/](../videos/raw/).

**How far to trust this.** It is one day of data. The ladder numbers were decoded from poe.ninja's undocumented feed, so treat percentages as approximate. Reddit quotes are single players. Wicker Man drop locations are datamined, not confirmed.

## Short version

- **Lightning Tendrils is having a very good league.** Players and creators report easy campaigns and fast boss kills, and one big creator switched his own starter to it on day one. The complaints are the ones we expected: squishy if you don't upgrade gear, and weak on a 4-link late in the campaign.
- **Penance Brand has almost no reports at all**, good or bad. About as many people play it as Tendrils Elementalist, but nobody is posting about it, and Goratha, who wrote its guide, started a different build. Nothing found changes the head-to-head.
- **The ladder is melee.** Static Strike is on one character in nine. The one wand attack build with real numbers is Kinetic Blast of Clustering Necromancer; see [Top builds and wand attack builds](#top-builds-and-wand-attack-builds).
- **The league mechanic is the main tip.** It hands out gear all through the campaign. Use it to fix your gear before and after the switch.

## Lightning Tendrils: gotchas and tips

**1. Most of the ladder runs Master T's version, not Peuget2's.** Among the 949 Tendrils Elementalists: Berek's Grip 38%, Marylene's Fallacy 37%, Xirgil's Crank 33%, Geofri's Crest 23%, a rare staff 23%. Peuget2's markers are rarer: Shaper of Winter 19%, a rare wand 21%, a rare shield 31%, Doedre's Damning 3%. Nick's version is still the one with the checked defences. It just means that when you look at other people's characters for ideas, most will be built differently.

**2. The strong pulse always crits, and people build around that.** Palsteron: "Stronger pulses always critically strike. Every third pulse is a strong pulse", and "this guaranteed crit cannot be reduced by anything". Two cheap consequences:

- Supports that lower crit chance cost little. Controlled Destruction is on 84% of ladder Tendrils Elementalists, next to Arcane Surge (95%), Infused Channelling (70%) and Added Lightning Damage (64%). Many levelling characters pair it with Elemental Overload (43%).
- Crit multiplier is worth more than crit chance. Marylene's Fallacy (huge multiplier, lower crit chance) is the amulet on 37% of them, and Master T prices it at "two or three chaos".

Peuget2 scales crit the normal way, with crit chance on the tree and Increased Critical Damage in the links. **Neither swap has been tested on his tree**, so treat them as things to try if damage feels low after the switch, not as corrections to his guide.

**3. Upgrade gear when you switch.** Both "squishy" reports came from people who kept campaign gear too long. Tuadh's racing character showed the same thing before launch (7.2K one-hit limit at level 76). The tankiness is in Peuget2's gear and tree.

**4. Get +1 lightning gems on the wand and the shield.** Palsteron: "Make sure that you grab plus one lightning on your wand and plus one lightning on your shield", and if they don't drop, "just buy it", because they are "getting absolutely printed by the league mechanic". He also says a Tabula Rasa is about 4c because so many are dropping.

**5. Wicker Man parts for Tendrils** (Master T, from datamined data; drop sources "allegedly"):

| Slot | Bonus | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|
| Head | 12% more maximum lightning damage | Plain damage. Linked to the Act 1 totem boss |
| Arm | 40% increased damage against chilled enemies | Shaper of Winter chills everything |
| Leg | +5% to maximum chill effect | Chill is also Peuget2's defence layer. Linked to Merveil |
| Head, defensive option | Regenerate 4% life per second if hit recently | Peuget2 picked this one out for the build |

## Winter Orb

Added Nov 14, later the same day, when Nick asked whether it is as bad as predicted ([ladder data](../news/raw/2026-11-14-poeninja-winter-orb.md), [Reddit](../news/raw/2026-11-14-reddit-winter-orb.md)).

**Almost nobody is playing it, and nobody has reported how it plays.** The collapse in numbers is measured. How weak it actually is, is not.

| | Day one of 3.29 | Day one of 3.30 |
|---|---|---|
| Winter Orb characters on the ladder | 13,652 of 72,026 (19.0%) | 421 of 76,243 (0.55%) |
| Rank among skills | Most-played skill of 3.29 (day-one rank not pulled) | 42nd |
| Elementalist / Occultist | 12,744 / 530 | 263 / 69 |

For scale, Lightning Tendrils is 14th with 1,373 and Penance Brand 29th with 827. The top three are Static Strike, Shockwave and Righteous Fire.

- **No 3.30 video exists.** None of its creators (Fubgun, ronarray, SoloHood, Lexyu, Zizaran) has uploaded a Winter Orb video since launch, and poebuilds.cc lists nothing newer than three months.
- **One Reddit mention**, explaining a price: "Last league a fuckton of people started aoe cold spells (specifically Winter Orb). That's no longer the case due to nerfs and probably the new boss-oriented league mechanic." The archive's text search was thin for this window, so more may exist.
- **The few who play it look normal.** Most of the 263 Elementalists hold the starter wand (Mystic Refractor, 60%) and have their third or fourth lab, so they are in maps. About a third of the 69 Occultists already wear Ralakesh's Impatience, which is the power-charge endgame version. So the top end of the build still draws a few fast players.
- **What we had on paper:** about 30% less boss damage on the starter wand after the nerf, still roughly 5–6M on starter gear. Fubgun said "30 to 40% weaker". Nothing since launch confirms or contradicts those numbers.

**Reading:** the prediction that it would fall out of favour came true in full. Whether it feels bad in play is still unknown. It stays a possible later swap for the pop, with weak evidence, and its gear should be cheap because so few are buying (my inference from the Reddit comment, not a checked price).

- **Static Strike is the league.** One character in nine uses it, and Guardian, its main ascendancy, went from 1.7% of the ladder to 7.4%. ### Wand attack builds

A wand and shield is the most common weapon setup in the league (23% of characters), but most of those are casters and minion builds. Three wand attack skills have real numbers:

**1. Kinetic Blast of Clustering Necromancer: yes, this one is doing something.** 909 characters, 861 of them Necromancers. Its share of the ladder is almost three times what it was on day one of 3.29, it is the third most common skill on a wand and shield (behind Absolution and its Vaal version), and its hundred highest characters are all level 91 to 95.

- The ladder version is the one on the shortlist: Spellslinger on 99%, Volatile Dead of Seething on 62% and plain Volatile Dead on 32%, Sacred Wisps on 93%, Returning Projectiles and Fork.
- **About half add ballista totems** (Ballista Totem 49%, Focused Ballista 47%). Both guide authors use them for single target only, which is this build's known weak side. Nick is fine with that; his rule is no clearing with two skills.
- Uniques: Doedre's Tenure 64%, Valako's Sign 55%, Eber's Unification 42%, Anathema 20%, Crown of the Inward Eye 14%.
- Player reports are few: "kboc necro leveling kinetic blast/fusillade/bolt and the mechanic is giving such outrageous loot that it's going very smoothly", and a Static Strike player who had a bad start is "swapping to KBoC Necro tomorrow".
- Necromancer is a Witch ascendancy, so for Nick it is a respec on the same character (an earlier version of this page wrongly said a second character). It is rank 5 on the [shortlist](../builds/wand-and-spell-shortlist.md), and it is compared with Tendrils in [kboc-necromancer-vs-lightning-tendrils.md](../builds/kboc-necromancer-vs-lightning-tendrils.md).

**2. Kinetic Blast Elementalist: popular, but the wand is only the trigger.** 581 Elementalists use Kinetic Blast, about 9% of the class. They are not wanders. The Poet's Pen is on 566 of the 816 Kinetic Blast characters, with Reap, Corrupting Fever and Brutality on 69% and Haemophilia gloves on 66%, and the Kinetic Blast tooltip on the top hundred is a median 17,000 DPS. This is the **Phys DoT Elementalist** that Fubgun ranked 2nd of 18 starters ("exceptional scaling", "really really good map clearing") and Ruetoo calls an "autobomber": you attack with the wand, The Poet's Pen casts its socketed spells for you (Reap and Exsanguinate of Transmission are the common ones on these characters), and Haemophilia makes bleeding enemies explode.

- Why it is worth a look: a wand attack, a trigger build and screen pop, on the class Nick already plays, so it is a respec. One Reddit comment: "Since poet's pen costs pennies you can make a very cheap physdot blaster".
- Why it is not on the shortlist: the damage is physical damage over time. It has not been read or rated here; the guide the creators point to is [Ventrua's from 3.29](https://youtu.be/d20_HuZjLVs).
- The hundred highest Kinetic Blast characters are level 91 to 100. The data does not say whether the level 100 is this build or a Deadeye wander.

**3. Kinetic Fusillade: ballista totems.** 769 characters, 78% Hierophant, 90% on Ballista Totem Support. Its share fell from 1.7% to 1.3%. Nick ruled the skill out as janky and does not play totems.

## The league mechanic and early maps

**Campaign**

- "League mechanic is crazy. Do it as much as possible" (Palsteron). One player was resistance capped from Act 2; others report movement-speed boots, Tabulas and levelling uniques from it.
- **Fated items** carry the modifier named on the Fate card, at a higher tier than normal. Pick the cards that name a slot you need.
- **Cards with underlined reward text need two of the same card** in the zone, or they do nothing (Palsteron).
- You get two Fate slots in the campaign. The third unlocks after Kitava in Act 10.
- **Viscera (Wicker Man parts) are a chance drop**, and more Fates raise the chance. If none ever drop and the Wicker Man will not open, several players fixed it by ctrl-clicking it or by buying one cheap part on trade.
- The cauldron blocks skills. Fight the pack from the other side.
- It is easy to press confirm on the boss with no Fates slotted. One player did it three times.
- The order you slot copy Fates in changes the rewards (three separate Reddit posts; details not checked).
- There are no Fated rewards for attack wands, one player says. That only matters while levelling with Power Siphon.

**Maps**

- **Fated map bosses hit hard.** "It's a bit rippy in maps so far. I had to stop picking it up just so I could progress." Start with one Fate and add more as gear allows.
- **Avoid the Bestiary region at first.** Shaped regions force Threads onto your maps, and the beast one is overtuned: "Random beasts in t5 maps were way harder than red map bosses." One player suggests leaving a region's objective undone to keep it at one Thread.
- **Bosses in the middle of a map mean backtracking.** Drop a portal at the boss room, or run linear maps such as City Square, Jungle Valley, Dunes and Strand.
- **Atlas (Fubgun):** take the shrine nodes first. Five shrines a map, two buffs each, at 90% increased effect: he expects early progression to be "really smooth and really really easy for almost any build". Then map nodes, then Eldritch altars on Dunes or Jungle Valley ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_mMOrBJzETM)).

## GGG since launch

Three hotfixes on Nov 13. None touches either build, the Fates or drop rates. One fixed abyss monsters dealing far too much physical damage over time. Servers were laggy and dropping connections on day one.
