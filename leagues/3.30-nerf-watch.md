## Mid-3.29 changes (none nerfed player builds)

- **3.29.1 (Jul 30):** permanent-merc damage penalty now tapers in (none before level 45, full at 83); attack-merc damage scaling raised; mercs got Golden Lantern quantity/rarity and slightly more supports; Coursing Current can support minions. ([forum](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3991633), [poedb](https://poedb.tw/Version_3.29.1))
- **~Jul 29 stealth change (unverified):** Corpse Pact and Devour stopped working on shattered enemies (affects Winter Orb).
- **3.29.2 (~Aug 7):** enemy merc skills nerfed; Offering blessings now apply to mercs.
- **3.29.3 (Aug 12):** more enemy merc nerfs; merc Wither on Hit fixed (a buff). ([forum](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/3996516))

**Takeaway:** GGG never touched Luminary, Flame Link or merc gear during 3.29 and buffed mercs twice, so **mercenary/Luminary builds are the prime 3.30 nerf target**. Winter Orb is the most likely player-skill target.
