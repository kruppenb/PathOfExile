Leveling sheet · Elementalist (Witch)

From Master T's 3.30 guide (his PoB, trimmed to Act 1 through Late Maps, saved in the repo) · made 2026-11-14 · quest data may change in a new league

Lightning Tendrils Elementalist, staff version · 3.30 (Master T's PoB. Energy shield and armour, crit multiplier without crit chance.)
Joining at level 55–60 from Peuget2's route: 45 refund points from the Power Siphon tree, or 40 if you already did his Tendrils swap. No ascendancy refund. Skip cards A1 to A6 and follow from the A7 card, "Act 7 Staff Swap". The passive rows on the cards count from Master T's own earlier tree, so use the list below or load the PoB code in the box.
Tree at the swap, 65 points: keep Arcanist's Dominion, Cruel Preparation, Faith and Steel, Golem Commander, Practical Application, Purity of Flesh and Sovereignty. Add Zealot's Oath, Crackling Speed, Enigmatic Defence, Lightning Walker, Mental Rapidity, Might, Mystic Bulwark and Safeguard. Refund the wand notables and the rest. Towards maps: Divine Shield, Wicked Ward, Whirling Barrier, Sanctum of Thought, Static Blows, Influence.
PoB, starter version: copy the code in the box, then in Path of Building open Import/Export Build, paste it where it asks for a URL or code, and press Import. It is Master T's PoB cut down to his four staff stages: "Act 7 Staff Swap" (any war staff with block), then "Start of Maps", "End of Early Maps" and "Late Maps" (Xirgil's Crank). It opens on the first. Change stage with the Loadout dropdown at the top. His campaign stages before the swap, which use wands and then a sceptre and shield, are left out.
His full PoB with the endgame stages is the code on his Mobalytics guide page. He has not posted a pobb.in link.
Buy first, about 65c on Nov 14: Xirgil's Crank 6c (lvl 23), Marylene's Fallacy 23c (lvl 40), Berek's Grip 5c (lvl 20), Geofri's Crest 2c (lvl 53), Righteous Fire of Arcane Devotion gem 29c. No Crank yet: any war staff with attack block, crafted cast speed.
Refunding with gold: talk to Faustus, the Currency Exchange trader (Kingsmarch, or your hideout once invited), and choose "Respecialization". The tree opens in a mode where each refunded point costs gold. Quest refund points are free and Orbs of Regret are about 2c a point.
Rest of your gear: keep it. Only the staff and the amulet must change. The staff replaces wand and shield, so move their gems into it and check that resistances are still capped. You need Strength for the staff and the red gems (golems, Vitality, Arrogance, Less Duration), and Dexterity for Haste and Arctic Armour. Mana on rings and belt helps. Armour and energy shield bases can wait until maps. Put Righteous Fire of Arcane Devotion on last and drop it if it drains you.
One skill: Lightning Tendrils with Added Lightning Damage, Controlled Destruction and Arcane Surge. By maps Infused Channelling replaces Added Lightning Damage. Six-link: add Inspiration and Focused Channelling.
Move: Lightning Warp with Less Duration.
Always on: Righteous Fire of Arcane Devotion (switch it on in each map), Purity of Elements, Clarity and Vitality on Arrogance, three golems. From maps add Discipline and Wrath.
Bosses: Arcanist Brand with Conductivity, and Wave of Conviction for exposure.
Labs: you have Liege of the Primordial and Shaper of Winter. Take Mastermind of Discord third and Heart of Destruction fourth. Both guides end on these four.
Next upgrade: Duskdawn staff, about 1.2 div. The low-life stage after it (Coruscating Elixir, timeless jewel) is optional and not on this sheet. (Added Nov 14. See builds/lightning-tendrils-staff-vs-wand-shield.md.)
