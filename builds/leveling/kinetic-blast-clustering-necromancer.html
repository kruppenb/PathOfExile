Kinetic Blast of Clustering Necromancer · 3.30 (lifewithoutpants' 3.30 PoB. Lightning version first; chaos and Chaos Inoculation come later.)
Joining at level ~55 from the Tendrils route: 58 refund points from the Power Siphon tree (38 passives + 20 for the ascendancy), or 85 if you already switched to Tendrils. Run a Labyrinth, pick Necromancer, take Plaguebringer and Corpse Pact. Then follow the cards from A7 (level 51–55).
Lvl 31–68, one clear skill: Kinetic Blast with Fork, Sacred Wisps, Returning Projectiles and Added Lightning Damage. From 56 add Spellslinger with Volatile Dead, Desecrate and Spell Cascade: it fires by itself and feeds Corpse Pact, which is the attack speed.
Bosses only: Kinetic Bolt with Ballista Totem, Focused Ballista and Added Lightning Damage. Drop them in the arena, then attack. He does not use them while mapping.
Lvl 69+: buy Kinetic Blast of Clustering (about 5c on Nov 14) and a six-link. Second Spellslinger with Tornado and Bone Offering for block. Sniper's Mark on Mark On Hit.
Gear: Kinetic Wand with flat damage, crit and mana (the league mechanic gives no attack wands), spirit shield with spell damage, Doedre's Tenure or Prismweave (1c), Shavronne's Revelation (9c). Flat mana on every rare: mana is damage. Anoint Aggressive Bastion.
Weak side: bosses, until Whispers of Infinity (about 12 div on Nov 14, expected to fall). After it: chaos version, then Chaos Inoculation near level 90.
Labs: Plaguebringer, Corpse Pact, Mistress of Sacrifice, Essence Glutton. (Added Nov 14. See builds/kboc-necromancer-vs-lightning-tendrils.md.)
