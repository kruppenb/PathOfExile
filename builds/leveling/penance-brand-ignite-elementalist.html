Leveling sheet · Elementalist (Witch)

From https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=42t9yK-9_Go · made 2026-11-12 · quest data may change in a new league

Penance Brand ignite · 3.30 league start (Goratha's 3.29 PoB; 3.30 didn't touch Penance Brand. His Kinetic wand and Holy Flame Totem options are cut.)
Lvl 1–28: Rolling Magma + Flame Wall (fire, so ignite and fire nodes pay off early), Shield Charge to move.
Lvl 28, A3: buy Penance Brand after Sever the Right Hand, 4L in the helmet with Swiftbrand. This is the only skill switch. It gets strong at the normal lab: Shaper of Flames makes the brand ignite on its own, so you need no ignite chance.
Weapon: Nycta's Lantern (lvl 41) carries you into maps. Then Obliteration, but only once your gloves have ignite spread, or its kill explosions won't ignite. Aegis Aurora shield for defence in maps.
Bosses: single target is weakest from the first maps to four Voidstones. Wear Mark of Submission with Flammability from the first maps. On bosses, hold Nycta's Lantern or Rebuke of the Vaal and put Spellblade in for Swiftbrand; start leveling Spellblade early, it needs to be high level. Obliteration is for maps only. (Added Nov 13 from animeprincess's 3.30 guide.)
3.30 update (Goratha): he calls the build "fine at bossing", "not really a bossing build", and this league is built around map bosses, so keep a fallback in mind (Lightning Tendrils or Conduit, see the shortlist). His faster option from 28 to maps is Armageddon Brand; Penance Brand as planned here still works. (Added Nov 13, launch day.)
Respec: about 19 points around Act 7, when you go onto the mapping tree (drops the lightning leveling nodes). Have Orbs of Regret ready.
