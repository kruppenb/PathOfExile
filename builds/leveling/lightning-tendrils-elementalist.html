Leveling sheet · Elementalist (Witch)

From Peuget2's 3.30 guide (his PoB, saved in the repo) · made 2026-11-13 · quest data may change in a new league

Lightning Tendrils · 3.30 league start (Peuget2's 3.30 PoB, wand + shield. He says the patch notes changed nothing that matters for it.)
Lvl 1–12: Kinetic Bolt. His Holy Flame Totem is optional, skip it. Buy Lightning Tendrils from Nessa after the first quest if you want to try it now; he calls it "kind of slow" this early, and Master T calls Act 1 "the roughest feeling act".
Lvl 12–55: Power Siphon + Sacred Wisps on a wand, heralds on. Liege of the Primordial (golems) at the first lab.
Lvl ~55, second lab: switch to Lightning Tendrils with Added Lightning Damage, Inspiration, Increased Critical Damage and Infused Channelling. Take Shaper of Winter. This is the only skill switch.
Play: stand still and channel. The big pulse always crits, so cast speed and crit multiplier are the stats. Shield Charge and Flame Dash move you. Bosses use the same setup, no weapon or gem swap.
Gear: spell wand (cast speed, spell damage, spell crit) and a spirit shield with life; armour/ES body armour; Doedre's Damning from early maps; gloves with lightning exposure later. Watch mana: Inspiration stays in the links.
Target at two Voidstones (level 92): 4.6K life + 1.9K ES, 78% / 67% block, 25.8K physical max hit, 5.4M DPS with Convergence up.
Labs: Liege of the Primordial, Shaper of Winter, Bastion of Elements. His early-maps tree then drops Bastion for Mastermind of Discord and adds Heart of Destruction at the fourth lab.
Hedge: at level 28 buy Penance Brand + Swiftbrand from Clarissa and level them in spare sockets, in case you want the pop back later. (Added Nov 13, launch day. See builds/penance-brand-vs-lightning-tendrils.md.)
Gems: int dex str dashed = support Nametake = pick as quest reward, rest = buy · faded = already hadPassives: click left → right; +3Notable = 3 small nodes on the way. Map: gold = this act.Hover anything for details.
