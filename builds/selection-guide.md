# How to pick a league starter

A league starter has to do three jobs with almost no currency: **level fast, map smoothly to red maps, and kill early pinnacle bosses**.
Use this checklist to score candidates. Then fill in [nick-profile.md](nick-profile.md) so recommendations fit how Nick plays.

## Hard requirements

1. **Works with self-found gear.** Core uniques must be cheap (or absent). If the build needs a 5+ divine unique to function, it's not a starter.
2. **Damage scales from gem levels / tree, not gear.** DoT, minions, totems, traps/mines, and spells with high base damage scale this way; attack builds lean on weapons.
3. **Defences online early.** Life or ES pool + capped res + one more layer (armour/evasion/block/suppression/regen/leech).
4. **Not nerfed in this patch.** Check the 3.30 patch notes.

## Scoring (1–5 each)

| Criterion | What "5" looks like |
|---|---|
| Leveling | Main skill or a smooth leveler works from Act 1, no awkward swap |
| Clear speed | One-button clear of a T16 at moderate budget |
| Single target | Kills Fated area bosses and Voidstone bosses comfortably (big deal in 3.30) |
| Tankiness | Survives juiced maps and boss one-shots with moderate gear |
| Budget scaling | Gets much stronger with each divine; has a clear upgrade path |
| SSF-friendliness | Doesn't rely on trade for key items (matters only if playing SSF) |
| Fun/playstyle fit | Matches Nick's preferences (melee vs ranged, buttons, minions or not) |
| League-mechanic synergy | Strong bosses → more Fate rewards |

## Archetype cheat-sheet

| Archetype | Starter? | Pros | Cons | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Righteous Fire | Excellent | Very tanky, cheap, simple | Slow-ish, regen-dependent maps are bad | RF Chieftain / Inquisitor |
| Damage over time (poison, ignite, cold DoT) | Excellent | Scales with gems/tree, safe | Ramp-up on bosses | Toxic Rain/Caustic Arrow Pathfinder, Cold DoT Trickster/Occultist, Essence Drain/Contagion |
| Totems / ballistas / traps / mines | Very good | Safe, strong single target | Placement playstyle | Explosive Arrow Ballista Hierophant, Glacial Cascade mines, Explosive Trap |
| Minions | Very good | Very safe, cheap | Slow, AI pathing, many buttons | Spectres/SRS/Holy Relic Necromancer, Animate Guardian, Absolution |
| Spell casters (hit) | Good | Fast clear | Squishier, need wand/staff upgrades | Winter Orb, Penance Brand, Arc, Spark |
| Bow attacks | Good | Fast clear | Needs a decent bow; boss damage needs gear | Lightning Arrow / Elemental Hit Deadeye |
| Melee attacks | Mixed | Tanky, fun | Gear-dependent damage; worst early | Cyclone Slayer, Boneshatter/Earthshatter Jugg, Facebreaker Champion |
| Strength/stat stackers, CoC, autobombers | Usually not | Huge ceilings | Need expensive pieces first | Strength Stacker Jugg, CoC builds |

## Process

1. Read patch notes → strike nerfed candidates from [candidates.md](candidates.md). (Done Nov 11: Winter Orb and Luminary/mercenary builds were the nerfs.)
2. Shortlist 3 from the candidates that fit Nick's profile.
3. Open each in PoB; check level-90 numbers with "budget" gear tier.
4. Watch a creator's 3.30 video for the top choice; check launch-day poe.ninja for early adoption.
5. Pick one **main** and one **backup** (in case of a stealth nerf or it feels bad by Act 5).
6. Generate a leveling guide with the `poe1-leveling-guide-builder` skill.
