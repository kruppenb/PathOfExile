# Kinetic Blast of Clustering Necromancer vs Lightning Tendrils

Written 2026-11-14, day two of 3.30, at Nick's request. He is levelling Lightning Tendrils on Peuget2's route (Power Siphon to about level 55, then Tendrils) and asked whether Kinetic Blast of Clustering ("KBoC") on a Necromancer is worth switching to. Necromancer is a Witch ascendancy, so this is a respec on the same character. Numbers come from creators' PoB files and videos, a day-one ladder snapshot and day-two prices. PoB is not updated for 3.30. Ratings are estimates on the shortlist's 1–5 scale.

## Verdict

**Stay on the Tendrils plan for now, and make the Necromancer your swap.** KBoC fits what you enjoy better: a wand attack, two Spellslinger triggers, real pop, and you keep moving. Tendrils is better at the two things you rank first, not dying and killing bosses early, and this league is built on bosses. KBoC's own guide authors say its single target is poor until one amulet, Whispers of Infinity, which costs about 12 div today. Redviles saw it fall from 10 div to about 2 div in three days in an earlier league.

**Try it for free first.** Kinetic Blast is a wand attack, so it works on the character you have now with no respec (see [Try it today](#try-it-today)). Ten minutes will tell you whether the clear is worth chasing.

**If the trial wins you over and you have not switched to Tendrils yet, that is the cheapest moment to go**: 58 refund points from the Power Siphon tree, against about 125 to 135 from maps. You would be trading safer, faster bosses now for the build you like more now.

## Your question about the totems

**You are right: the ballistas are for bosses only.** You clear with one skill, Kinetic Blast, and Spellslinger casts everything else for you.

- lifewithoutpants, on his Kinetic Bolt ballistas: "this is really good for single target on bosses. I don't really use it whilst I'm mapping", and "you get into the boss arena, you drop a bunch of kinetic bolt totems and then blast away". His PoB uses them from level 31 to 68 and drops them when the Clustering gem goes in.
- Redviles adds a ballista setup for single target, with a second Kinetic Blast of Clustering on Focused Ballista: "focus ballista attacks when you attack", and players told him "they regret not doing a second ballista totem".
- On the day-one ladder 49% of the 909 KBoC characters carry Ballista Totem Support and 47% Focused Ballista. So half the players boss with them and half do without.

## What the switch costs

One refund point per passive, five per ascendancy point, plus one Labyrinth run to pick Necromancer at the altar. Orbs of Regret are about 2c today (thin data) and a Divine Orb about 98c. Quest refund points and gold (in the passive screen) also pay for refunds.

| When you switch | Refund points | About |
|---|---|---|
| **Before the Tendrils switch**, from the Power Siphon tree (level ~55, two labs) | 38 + 20 = **58** | 1.2 div |
| Just after the Tendrils switch (level ~55) | 65 + 20 = 85 | 1.7 div |
| After the third lab (level ~68) | 72 + 30 = 102 | 2.1 div |
| Early maps (level ~80, four labs) | 84 + 40 = 124 | 2.5 div |
| Two Voidstones (level ~92) | 96 + 40 = 136 | 2.8 div |

From the Power Siphon tree you keep every wand notable (Wandslinger, Fusillade, Disintegration, Elder Power), because his KBoC tree takes the same ones. After the Tendrils switch those are gone and almost nothing is shared. The Tendrils switch itself costs 28 points, so Tendrils first and KBoC later costs about 150 to 165 points in total against 58 for going straight there.

## Try it today

No respec, no class change. On your current Elementalist:

1. Buy **Kinetic Blast** from Clarissa in Act 3 and put it where Power Siphon is. The Clustering version is 5c on trade if you want the real gem; the guides bring it in around level 69.
2. Links, in order of importance: **Sacred Wisps** and **Added Lightning Damage** (you have both), then **Fork** (Siosa, Act 3 library) and **Returning Projectiles** (Petarus and Vanja, Act 4).
3. For a boss, his levelling setup is Kinetic Bolt with Ballista Totem, Focused Ballista (both from Siosa) and Added Lightning Damage.

It will be weaker than the real thing: no Corpse Pact attack speed and no Clustering gem, which comes at level 69. The point is to see whether you like how it clears.

## If you switch: the checklist

- Refund points as in the table, then a Labyrinth run: Plaguebringer and Corpse Pact with two labs, Mistress of Sacrifice third, Essence Glutton fourth.
- Spellslinger with Desecrate and Volatile Dead feeds Corpse Pact, which is where the attack speed comes from. Set this up at the same time as the ascendancy.
- Kinetic Blast until level 69, then the Clustering gem: buy it (5c). He ran the lab "15 in a row" without being offered it.
- A Kinetic Wand with flat damage, crit and mana (his is essence-crafted). The league mechanic will not hand you one. A spirit shield with spell damage.
- Doedre's Tenure or Prismweave (1c each) for damage. Flat mana on every rare.
- Anoint Aggressive Bastion (Verdant, Violet, Black) on the amulet for block.
- Before Whispers of Infinity, bosses need the ballistas, Sniper's Mark and patience. After it: chaos version, then Chaos Inoculation near level 90.

## What would change the call

- **Standing still annoys you once you are on Tendrils.** Then switch at the next convenient point; the cost only rises by about 15 to 20 points per stage.
- **Whispers of Infinity drops to 2 or 3 div.** That removes KBoC's weak window, and it is the natural moment to swap.
- **Fated bosses stay easy on Tendrils and currency piles up.** Then the swap is a week-two treat, as the shortlist's [mid-budget section](wand-and-spell-shortlist.md#mid-budget-picks-week-2-swaps-on-the-same-character-20-div) already had it.
