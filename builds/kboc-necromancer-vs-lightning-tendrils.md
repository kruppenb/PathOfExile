# Kinetic Blast of Clustering Necromancer vs Lightning Tendrils

Written 2026-11-14, day two of 3.30, at Nick's request. He is levelling Lightning Tendrils on Peuget2's route (Power Siphon to about level 55, then Tendrils) and asked whether Kinetic Blast of Clustering ("KBoC") on a Necromancer is worth switching to. Necromancer is a Witch ascendancy, so this is a respec on the same character. Numbers come from creators' PoB files and videos, a day-one ladder snapshot and day-two prices. PoB is not updated for 3.30. Ratings are estimates on the shortlist's 1–5 scale.

## Verdict

**Stay on the Tendrils plan for now, and make the Necromancer your swap.** KBoC fits what you enjoy better: a wand attack, two Spellslinger triggers, real pop, and you keep moving. Tendrils is better at the two things you rank first, not dying and killing bosses early, and this league is built on bosses. KBoC's own guide authors say its single target is poor until one amulet, Whispers of Infinity, which costs about 12 div today.

**Updated Nov 14 evening: the swap does not have to wait for the amulet.** Only 24 of the 886 KBoC Necromancers on the ladder wear it, and the players without it are levelling faster than the Tendrils players. What they give up is boss damage and some sturdiness, and the sturdiness comes back with Chaos Inoculation, not with the amulet. See [Without Whispers of Infinity](#without-whispers-of-infinity-what-the-ladder-shows).

**Try it for free first.** Kinetic Blast is a wand attack, so it works on the character you have now with no respec (see [Try it today](#try-it-today)). Ten minutes will tell you whether the clear is worth chasing.

**If the trial wins you over and you have not switched to Tendrils yet, that is the cheapest moment to go**: 58 refund points from the Power Siphon tree, against about 125 to 135 from maps. You would be trading safer, faster bosses now for the build you like more now.

## Your question about the totems

**You are right: the ballistas are for bosses only.** You clear with one skill, Kinetic Blast, and Spellslinger casts everything else for you.

- lifewithoutpants, on his Kinetic Bolt ballistas: "this is really good for single target on bosses. I don't really use it whilst I'm mapping", and "you get into the boss arena, you drop a bunch of kinetic bolt totems and then blast away". His PoB uses them from level 31 to 68 and drops them when the Clustering gem goes in.
- Redviles adds a ballista setup for single target, with a second Kinetic Blast of Clustering on Focused Ballista: "focus ballista attacks when you attack", and players told him "they regret not doing a second ballista totem".
- On the day-one ladder 49% of the 909 KBoC characters carry Ballista Totem Support and 47% Focused Ballista. So half the players boss with them and half do without.

## Without Whispers of Infinity: what the ladder shows

Added Nov 14 evening, after Nick asked how the Necromancer does without the amulet and who has it. All of it is one poe.ninja snapshot from day two (62,168 characters). poe.ninja lists only the hundred highest-level characters of a search, so "top hundred" means those. Raw data: [KBoC with and without the amulet](../news/raw/2026-11-14-poeninja-kboc-whispers.md), [Tendrils](../news/raw/2026-11-14-poeninja-tendrils-defence.md), [amulet prices](../news/raw/2026-11-14-poewatch-whispers-of-infinity.md).

**Who has it: almost nobody.** 24 of the 886 KBoC Necromancers wear it (2.7%). In the whole league it is on 31 characters: those 24, five Viper Strike of the Mamba Assassins and two others. The 24 are the players who have already bought the rest of the expensive set: 21 also wear Shavronne's Revelation, 18 Crown of the Inward Eye and 13 Anathema, and 7 are on Chaos Inoculation.

**The build does well without it.**

| | Without the amulet (862 characters) | With it (24 characters) |
|---|---|---|
| Levels | Top hundred: 91 to 95, and 73 of them are 92 or higher | All 24: 78 to 95, median 91, and 11 are 92 or higher |
| Tooltip DPS, median | 360K (top hundred) | 474K (all 24) |
| Tooltip DPS, highest | 800K | 1.4M. Four of the twelve highest-level are above 1.0M |
| Ballistas for bosses | 51% | 21% |
| Chaos Inoculation | 9 characters, 1% | 7 characters, 29% |
| Common uniques | Doedre's Tenure 69%, Valako's Sign 59%, Eber's Unification 46% | Shavronne's Revelation 88%, Crown of the Inward Eye 75%, Anathema 54% |

The hundred highest-level Necromancers without the amulet have a median level of 92. The hundred highest-level Tendrils Elementalists have a median of 89 (85 to 94).

**What the amulet does.** It adds flat chaos damage to attacks for every 100 maximum mana, reduces maximum mana by 40 to 60% and makes skills cost energy shield. It is a damage item. On the ladder its wearers show about a third more tooltip damage at the median and close to double at the top, and some of that is the rest of their gear (my reading). In lifewithoutpants' PoB it appears only in the last item set.

**The chaos version does not need it.** The median top-hundred character without the amulet already deals 88% of its damage as chaos. That comes from a medium cluster jewel with Overwhelming Malice (Unholy Might on crit, which converts physical damage to chaos), the step lifewithoutpants' "Chaos" tree takes. An earlier version of this page tied the chaos version to the amulet, which was wrong.

**Reading it** (mine):

- **Tendrils is the sturdier of the two today, by a moderate margin.** About half again the EHP among the very best, about a sixth more across the top hundred, a higher physical max hit, several times the armour, and chaos resistance above zero. The life and ES pools are the same size.
- **Chaos Inoculation is what fixes the Necromancer, not the amulet.** The five CI characters sit at 70K to 106K EHP on about 6K to 7.5K ES. lifewithoutpants puts the CI swap near level 90.
- **Compare ladder with ladder, not with a creator's PoB.** Peuget2's PoB shows a 25.8K physical max hit and 259K EHP. The one top-fifteen character built like it (wand and shield, Doedre's Damning, 4.2K life + 1.5K ES at level 91) shows 10.8K and 94K on poe.ninja. So poe.ninja reads roughly 2.5 times lower than that PoB. Flasks are not the reason: poe.ninja switches all five flasks on (checked on Nov 14, see the Bone Offering paragraph below). Peuget2's PoB also ticks endurance charges, standing still, a 40% chill on the enemy (Shaper of Winter), Sand stance and full energy shield, and it is a finished two-Voidstone gear set, not a day-two character. I have not worked out which of these makes up the gap.
- **Tooltips compare loosely across skills.** Tendrils' tooltip counts every pulse. KBoC's is the one skill, without Volatile Dead, the ballistas or the Tornado hits. A fivefold gap still agrees with what KBoC's own guide authors say about bosses.
- **The Necromancers are about three levels ahead**, which fits the clear-speed gap. Reaching 94 or 95 by day two also suggests the thin defence is not costing the best players many deaths. The ladder counts neither deaths nor boss kills.

**Bone Offering is counted in these numbers.** Nick asked on Nov 14 whether the Necromancer's EHP includes its offering. It does. poe.ninja publishes the Path of Building file behind each character: for a level-95 KBoC Necromancer, Bone Offering sits in an enabled skill group, and PoB's block, EHP and physical max hit match poe.ninja's to the digit (75% / 75%, 88,710 and 8,595). Adding up that character's block by hand gives 48% attack block without the offering and 70% with it, against the 75% shown. The 66 KBoC Necromancers with no Bone Offering gem are level 79 to 88 and show 20% to 49% block and 4.5K to 22K EHP. So the first column of the table is the build with its offering up, and with all five flasks active (Rumi's Concoction included) and poe.ninja's default conditions switched on. That makes it the generous reading, not a low one. Raw data: [Bone Offering check](../news/raw/2026-11-14-poeninja-bone-offering-check.md).

**A side note on the Tendrils ladder.** Twelve of the fifteen highest-level Tendrils players use a staff (Duskdawn or Xirgil's Crank) with Marylene's Fallacy, Berek's Grip, Divine Shield, Zealot's Oath and Wicked Ward. Only two are on Peuget2's wand and shield, and those two are among the sturdiest (94K and 138K EHP). Looked into later on Nov 14: [staff version against wand and shield](lightning-tendrils-staff-vs-wand-shield.md), which recommends moving to the staff version.

**What this changes.** The amulet is the last upgrade, not the gate. If you swap, the order is: the Clustering gem and the cheap uniques (a few chaos), the Overwhelming Malice cluster jewel for the chaos version, Chaos Inoculation with 6K or more ES near level 90, then Whispers of Infinity. The reasons to stay on Tendrils are now boss damage and the sturdier character, not the price of one amulet.

## What the switch costs

One refund point per passive, five per ascendancy point, plus one Labyrinth run to pick Necromancer at the altar. Orbs of Regret are about 2c today (thin data) and a Divine Orb about 98c. Quest refund points and gold (at Faustus, "Respecialization") also pay for refunds.

| When you switch | Refund points | About |
|---|---|---|
| **Before the Tendrils switch**, from the Power Siphon tree (level ~55, two labs) | 38 + 20 = **58** | 1.2 div |
| Just after the Tendrils switch (level ~55) | 65 + 20 = 85 | 1.7 div |
| After the third lab (level ~68) | 72 + 30 = 102 | 2.1 div |
| Early maps (level ~80, four labs) | 84 + 40 = 124 | 2.5 div |
| Two Voidstones (level ~92) | 96 + 40 = 136 | 2.8 div |

From the Power Siphon tree you keep every wand notable (Wandslinger, Fusillade, Disintegration, Elder Power), because his KBoC tree takes the same ones. After the Tendrils switch those are gone and almost nothing is shared. The Tendrils switch itself costs 28 points, so Tendrils first and KBoC later costs about 150 to 165 points in total against 58 for going straight there.

## Try it today

No respec, no class change. On your current Elementalist:

1. Buy **Kinetic Blast** from Clarissa in Act 3 and put it where Power Siphon is. The Clustering version is 5c on trade if you want the real gem; the guides bring it in around level 69.
2. Links, in order of importance: **Sacred Wisps** and **Added Lightning Damage** (you have both), then **Fork** (Siosa, Act 3 library) and **Returning Projectiles** (Petarus and Vanja, Act 4).
3. For a boss, his levelling setup is Kinetic Bolt with Ballista Totem, Focused Ballista (both from Siosa) and Added Lightning Damage.

It will be weaker than the real thing: no Corpse Pact attack speed and no Clustering gem, which comes at level 69. The point is to see whether you like how it clears.

## If you switch: the checklist

- Refund points as in the table, then a Labyrinth run: Plaguebringer and Corpse Pact with two labs, Mistress of Sacrifice third, Essence Glutton fourth.
- Spellslinger with Desecrate and Volatile Dead feeds Corpse Pact, which is where the attack speed comes from. Set this up at the same time as the ascendancy.
- Kinetic Blast until level 69, then the Clustering gem: buy it (5c). He ran the lab "15 in a row" without being offered it.
- A Kinetic Wand with flat damage, crit and mana (his is essence-crafted). The league mechanic will not hand you one. A spirit shield with spell damage.
- Doedre's Tenure or Prismweave (1c each) for damage. Flat mana on every rare.
- Anoint Aggressive Bastion (Verdant, Violet, Black) on the amulet for block.
- Upgrade order: the chaos version as soon as you can buy a medium cluster jewel with Overwhelming Malice (it does not need the amulet), Chaos Inoculation near level 90 with 6K or more ES, and Whispers of Infinity last. Until the amulet, bosses need the ballistas, Sniper's Mark and patience.

## What would change the call

- **Standing still annoys you once you are on Tendrils.** Then switch at the next convenient point; the cost only rises by about 15 to 20 points per stage.
- **You can afford the Chaos Inoculation set, or Whispers of Infinity drops to a few div.** CI closes the defence gap and the amulet lifts boss damage. Either one shrinks KBoC's weak window, and the ladder shows the build maps well with neither.
- **Fated bosses stay easy on Tendrils and currency piles up.** Then the swap is a week-two treat, as the shortlist's [mid-budget section](wand-and-spell-shortlist.md#mid-budget-picks-week-2-swaps-on-the-same-character-20-div) already had it.
