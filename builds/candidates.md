# 3.30 starter candidates (general list)

**Status:** built on Nov 9 from 3.29 end-of-league data and pre-3.30 tier lists, then **checked against the 3.30 patch notes on Nov 11** ([GGG](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/4013680)). None are 3.30-tested.
Sources are mostly RMT-shop blogs and aggregators, so weigh the "consensus" column more than any single tier.
This is the general meta list. Nick's own picks and their ranking are in [wand-and-spell-shortlist.md](wand-and-spell-shortlist.md).

Legend: ✅ = appears on several lists · ⚠ = nerf risk or gear concerns · ↑ / ↓ / = is what the 3.30 notes did to it. "Post-patch top 10" is [The Divine Ledger's](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xaa6xKxAdFw) re-ranking of 3.29's most-played starters (popularity and retention, nerfed builds dropped).

## Higher ceiling / not ideal day 1
- **Luminary "Flame Link mercenary"** (Scion / Luminary): called the build of 3.29. **Nerfed in 3.30 as expected**: Golden Glory's light-radius effect on mercenary link buffs is halved, Flame Link's life-based fire damage is 3% (was 5%) with more flat damage, and mercenaries deal up to 20% less attack damage at level 83.
- **Strength Stacker Juggernaut** (Glacial Hammer of Shattering): great later, needs gear. Not checked against the notes.
- **Herald Stacker Elementalist autobomber**: cheap start but scaling needs ~50 div. Heralds are not in the notes.
- **CoC Forbidden Rite**: not online until high 80s. Not in the notes.
- **Cyclone (of Tumult) Slayer**: popular endgame, VizzarD has a starter guide. Not in the notes; #3 on the post-patch top 10.

## New or changed in 3.30
- **New gems:** Fireball of Radiation (transfigured: the fireball leaves a fire damage-over-time debuff in an area) and Arcane Link (links you to an allied player, who gains lightning damage based on your mana; if they die, you die).
- **Buffed ascendancies:** Trickster (Heartstopper, One Step Ahead, Spellbreaker), Warden (five notables), Pathfinder (flask notables). **Reliquarian** notables were swapped for a new set (see [the league file](../leagues/3.30-covens-of-umbra.md#reliquarian-scion-notables-for-330)).
- **Ruthless-only:** the Occultist, Elementalist, Hierophant, Chieftain, Slayer, Champion, Saboteur and Guardian lines in the notes. They don't apply to trade league.
- Everything else is in [the nerf watch](../leagues/3.30-nerf-watch.md#result-3300-patch-notes-posted-nov-10-2026).

## Mercenary note
Mercenaries (reworked in 3.29) can add a lot of damage. Early picks that sources liked: Fallen Reverend, Blade Ambusher; Kineticist for clear. **3.30 nerfed them:** up to 20% less attack damage at level 83, −30% resistances in level 46–67 areas and −60% above, Kalandra's Touch banned on them, and the Kineticist's Kinetic Blast has 150% Arcane Might (was 200%).
