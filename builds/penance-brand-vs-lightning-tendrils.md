# Penance Brand ignite vs Lightning Tendrils

Written 2026-11-13, launch day, at Nick's request: an in-depth comparison of only these two. Both are Elementalists on the same Witch. Numbers come from creators' PoB files and videos for 3.29 and 3.30; nobody has played 3.30 yet, and PoB is not updated for it. Ratings are estimates on the shortlist's 1–5 scale. Full sourcing for both builds is in [shortlist-reference.md](shortlist-reference.md#pick-details-full-notes).

## Verdict

**Start Lightning Tendrils.** It wins the two things Nick ranks highest, not dying and killing bosses early, in a league whose mechanic is a harder boss in every map. It is hit damage, not a DoT. And it is the one he wants to play, which matters over a whole league.

**What that costs:** the pop, some clear speed, and the freedom to move while the damage happens. Penance Brand is still the better mapper and the only one of the two with chain explosions. On the shortlist's score Penance Brand is ahead, 9 to 9.5 against 8.5, and **all of that gap is the Pop rating**. Take Pop out and Tendrils leads 8 to 7.

**If pop is what keeps you playing in week 2, pick Penance Brand instead.** That is the one honest reason to.

## How each one plays

**Penance Brand ignite.** You throw a brand at a pack and keep running. It builds energy, the last pulses ignite, and the ignite jumps from monster to monster. Before Lost Unity the brand takes about two seconds to go off, and Swiftbrand is the fix while mapping. The damage keeps ticking while you dodge, which is its real advantage in a messy fight. On bosses the creator's plan is a second weapon (Nycta's Lantern or Rebuke of the Vaal) and swapping Swiftbrand for a high-level Spellblade. With a Fated boss in most maps that swap will come up a lot. That last sentence is my reading, not a creator's.

**Lightning Tendrils.** This is the Palpatine build: you plant your feet, hold the button, and lightning pours out in a cone. Cast speed and crit multiplier are what you scale, because the strong pulse always crits. Shield Charge and Flame Dash move you between packs. There is nothing to swap for bosses: the mapping setup is the boss setup. The price is in the creator's own words, twice: "you need to stand still". When a boss makes you move, your damage is zero until you stop again, so the paper gap over Penance Brand will be smaller in fights with a lot of dodging. I have not measured by how much.

## Bossing, in a league built on bosses

3.30's mechanic lets you put up to three Fates on a map's boss. Fated bosses are harder, pay better, and drop the Viscera that feed the Wicker Man, which is character power. So early boss strength compounds this league.

Penance Brand's creators are open about it. animeprincess: "the hardest point in the game is going to be right now from maps until you get all your four void stones". Goratha, yesterday: "This is a bossing league. I think it's fine at bossing. It's just not really a bossing build". Fubgun ranks it 14th of 18 starters as "not as strong early".

Peuget2 says he built his Tendrils version with the new boss mechanic in mind. Tuadh's Tendrils practice run was the fastest of every pick on the shortlist to kill Eater and Exarch.

## Staying alive

Both builds cap or nearly cap block and both show big EHP numbers, and those numbers flatter them, because block and recovery do nothing against the one hit that gets through. The one-hit limit is the honest comparison: about 11–13K physical for Penance Brand against 25.8K for Tendrils at the same stage. Spicysushi's bar for this league is "around 35 to 60k", so neither is safe on starter gear; Tendrils is much closer. Peuget2 also counts on chill from Shaper of Winter for 20% less damage taken.

**One warning.** The tankiness belongs to Peuget2's tree and gear, not to the skill. Tuadh's level-76 racing character on the same skill has 24.6K EHP and a 7.2K one-hit limit, and he says "it's falling off a little now". Follow Peuget2's version.

## Levelling

- **Penance Brand:** Rolling Magma and Flame Wall to 28, then the brand with Swiftbrand, Shaper of Flames at the first lab, a respec of about 19 points around Act 7. Goratha's faster 3.30 option is Armageddon Brand from 28.
- **Lightning Tendrils:** opinions differ on when it feels good. Master T and Tuadh use it from level 1; Master T calls Act 1 "the roughest feeling act". Peuget2 calls it "kind of slow" early and levels with Kinetic Bolt, then **Power Siphon with Sacred Wisps** until the second lab. The Reddit author finds it "almost unplayable" until the area from Heart of Destruction. Liege of the Primordial is the first lab on every Tendrils route.

**My suggestion:** follow Peuget2's route on the [new sheet](leveling/lightning-tendrils-elementalist.html), and buy Lightning Tendrils from Nessa after the first quest for a Wisdom scroll to try the fantasy straight away. If the cone feels too small, Power Siphon carries you to 55, and Nick likes Power Siphon anyway.

## Changing your mind

Both are Elementalists, so it is a respec, never a new character. One refund point per passive, five per ascendancy point.

| Switch | Refund points |
|---|---|
| Penance Brand to Tendrils, in the campaign (~level 55) | about 52 |
| Penance Brand to Tendrils, from the mapping tree | about 78 (61 to Tuadh's simpler tree) |
| Tendrils to Penance Brand, from maps | 73 to 99 |

Armour and resistance rares carry over. Weapon and shield do not: Tendrils is crit and wants a spell wand and a spirit shield, Penance Brand wants its own weapon and Aegis Aurora.

**Cheap hedge:** at level 28 buy Penance Brand and Swiftbrand from Clarissa and level them in spare sockets. If the pop is missed in week 2, the gems are ready, and the switch back costs the Orbs of Regret above plus a weapon and Aegis Aurora.

## What would change the call

- Standing still turns out to annoy you more than slow bosses would. You will know by the end of Act 1.
- Lost Unity turns out cheap and plentiful this league. Then Penance Brand's pop and feel arrive early and its case gets stronger.
- Early reports show Fated bosses are full of forced movement. That hurts a stand-still channel more than a brand.
