# Lightning Tendrils: the staff version vs the wand and shield version

Written 2026-11-14, day two of 3.30, at Nick's request. He is levelling Lightning Tendrils Elementalist on Peuget2's route (Power Siphon to about level 55, then Tendrils on a wand and shield). The day-two ladder showed that most Tendrils players, and twelve of the fifteen highest-level ones, use a staff instead. That is Master T's version. This page compares the two so Nick can move towards the right one.

Sources: both authors' PoB files and guide videos, one poe.ninja ladder snapshot from day two (62,168 characters, 841 Lightning Tendrils Elementalists) and poe.watch prices from the same day. PoB is not updated for 3.30. Raw data is linked at the bottom.

## Verdict

**Move to the staff version.** Three reasons, in order of weight:

1. **It is what the ladder settled on.** 74% of Tendrils Elementalists use a staff and 17% a wand and shield. About 30 staff characters are level 90 or higher, against 7 with a shield.
2. **Its results are steadier.** The ten highest-level players on the 6c staff all show 44K or more EHP and 1.5M or more tooltip DPS. The shield players have the same median defence but a much wider spread, from 23K to 138K EHP and from 0.8M to 4.3M DPS.
3. **It is cheaper to make work.** It ignores crit chance, so it needs no power charges, Diamond Flask or Assassin's Mark. The whole starting kit is about 65c.

**It costs you two things.** Movement is Lightning Warp instead of Shield Charge, and you run a Righteous Fire buff. Its later low-life stage (Coruscating Elixir, a timeless jewel) is the complicated part of Master T's guide, and it is optional: almost nobody on the ladder has reached it and they are level 90 to 94 without it.

**Now is the cheapest moment to move**: 45 refund points from the Power Siphon tree, or 40 if you have already done Peuget2's Tendrils swap, against about 60 from maps.

## What the ladder shows

One poe.ninja snapshot, day two. poe.ninja lists the hundred highest-level characters of a search, and the lines below are its own calculation for the highest-level characters wearing each item, so the columns compare with each other.

**Who plays what.** Of 841 Lightning Tendrils Elementalists: staff 623 (74%), wand and shield 143 (17%), mace or sceptre and shield 54 (6%), other 21. Xirgil's Crank is on 343 characters, a rare staff on 209 (mostly still levelling, median level 74) and Duskdawn on 70. Of the fifteen highest-level Tendrils characters, twelve use a staff.

| | Xirgil's Crank (10 highest, level 90–94) | Duskdawn (6 highest, level 91–94) | Rare shield (15 highest, level 87–93) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Life and ES | 2.2K to 2.7K life + 2.8K to 4.4K ES | 1.9K to 3.2K life + 2.4K to 3.4K ES | 2.7K to 4.8K life + 0.2K to 1.5K ES (one is on Chaos Inoculation) |
| EHP | 44K to 83K, median 58K | 39K to 104K, median 75K | 23K to 138K, median 56K |
| Physical max hit | 7.0K to 11.3K, median 8.9K | 4.6K to 10.0K, median 8.5K | 4.6K to 14.3K, median 8.0K |
| Armour | 4.8K to 19.7K, median 13.5K | 7.8K to 17.2K | 1.5K to 8.6K, with one at 21.9K |
| Block / spell block | 63–68% / 62–74% | 75% / 73–78% | 49–78% / 63–78% |
| Tooltip DPS | 1.5M to 3.0M, median 2.2M | 1.7M to 4.3M, median 2.5M | 0.8M to 4.3M, median 1.6M |
| Level 90 or higher | 15 of 343 | 12 of 70 | 7 of 187 |
| Righteous Fire gem | All 10 | All 6 | 1 of 15 |

**Reading it** (mine):

- **Defence is close.** Median EHP and physical max hit are about the same. The staff players get there with energy shield and armour, the shield players with life and block.
- **The staff version is far more consistent.** Nobody in the Xirgil's Crank top ten is below 44K EHP or 1.5M DPS, on a 6c staff. The shield column has the best single character (138K EHP, 4.2M DPS, on Chaos Inoculation with expensive uniques) and also the weakest ones (23K and 25K EHP, 0.8M DPS). A version that needs crit chance and good rares punishes average gear.
- **The crit version's ceiling is real.** Four shield characters show 3.6M to 4.3M, above every Xirgil's Crank character. They are the well-geared minority.
- **Nobody is using the complicated late stage yet.** One of the sixteen staff characters has Pain Attunement and a Coruscating Elixir. The rest are on the simple setup.
- **None of the 31 characters uses Cast while Channelling.**
- The ladder counts neither boss kills nor deaths.

One more thing the shield column shows: Peuget2's exact setup is a minority even among shield players. Doedre's Damning is on 17% of them, Increased Critical Damage on 43%, and 49% have already gone to Controlled Destruction, the staff version's no-crit support.

## What it costs to move

Refund points measured between the two authors' trees, one per passive.

| Where you are (Peuget2's tree) | To Master T's tree | Refund points |
|---|---|---|
| Power Siphon tree, before the Tendrils swap (level ~55) | Act 7 Staff Swap | **45** (Peuget2's own Tendrils swap costs 28) |
| Just after Peuget2's Tendrils swap (level ~55) | Act 7 Staff Swap | 40 |
| After the third lab (level ~68) | Start of Maps | 49, plus 10 if you took Bastion of Elements |
| Early maps (level ~80) | End of Early Maps | 58 |
| Two Voidstones (level ~92) | Late Maps | 61 |

**The ascendancy needs no refund** if you have not taken Bastion of Elements. Both guides end on Liege of the Primordial, Shaper of Winter, Mastermind of Discord and Heart of Destruction. You have the first two. Take Mastermind of Discord at the third lab and Heart of Destruction at the fourth.

Orbs of Regret are about 2c each on thin data, so 45 points is under 1 div. Unused quest refund points and gold pay for part of it.

Going straight to the staff tree now costs 45 points. Doing Peuget2's swap now and moving in maps costs 28 and then about 60.

## If you move: what to do at level 55 to 60

The one-page version of this section, with every later stage of the tree and gems, is the [staff sheet](leveling/lightning-tendrils-staff-elementalist.html) made from Master T's PoB.

**Buy** (prices from Nov 14):

| Item | Price | Wearable from | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Xirgil's Crank | 6c | level 23 | 35% attack block, +1 to all spell gems, spell damage, ES, and a chance to start ES recharge when you block |
| Marylene's Fallacy | 23c | level 40 | +210 to 240% crit multiplier, culling strike. The crit-chance penalty does not touch the guaranteed pulse |
| Berek's Grip | 5c | level 20 | Life leech against shocked enemies, lightning damage, resistances |
| Geofri's Crest | 2c | level 53 | 20 to 30% of all four resistances, chaos included |
| Righteous Fire of Arcane Devotion (gem) | 29c | | The damage buff. 16% of Crank players use the plain Righteous Fire gem instead |

That is about 65c. The staff, ring and helmet alone are 13c.

**Tree.** Follow Master T's "Act 7 Staff Swap" tree. From the Power Siphon tree you refund the wand notables (Wandslinger, Fusillade, Disintegration, Elder Power) and Deep Wisdom, Disciple of the Forbidden, Divine Judgement, Heart and Soul, Instability, Overcharge and Searing Heat. You take Zealot's Oath, Crackling Speed, Enigmatic Defence, Lightning Walker, Mental Rapidity, Might, Mystic Bulwark and Safeguard. On the way to maps add Divine Shield, Wicked Ward, Whirling Barrier, Sanctum of Thought, Static Blows and Influence.

**Links in Act 7** (his PoB):

- Lightning Tendrils, Added Lightning Damage, Controlled Destruction, Arcane Surge. Infused Channelling replaces Added Lightning Damage by maps.
- Righteous Fire of Arcane Devotion with Haste.
- Lightning Warp with Less Duration.
- Lightning, Flame and Stone golems, with Arctic Armour. A Chaos Golem gem sits in a spare socket to level and is summoned later; he skips the extra golem node at league start.
- Conductivity, Wave of Conviction and Arcanist Brand, with Purity of Elements.
- Clarity and Vitality on Arrogance.
- Flasks: Granite, Basalt, Silver, Quicksilver and a life flask.

**By the start of maps** Discipline and Wrath come in, and by the end of early maps the six-link is Tendrils, Controlled Destruction, Arcane Surge, Infused Channelling, Inspiration and Focused Channelling.

## What I have not checked

- How Righteous Fire of Arcane Devotion is kept up, and whether mana is comfortable before Mastermind of Discord. In Master T's order that notable comes at the second lab and gives mana regeneration; in yours it comes at the third.
- Whether Lightning Warp feels acceptable to Nick. It is a delayed teleport, and Nick dislikes clunky mechanics.
