## Progression path (stable across leagues)

1. **Finish the campaign → white maps (T1–5).** Complete each new map once for atlas bonus and atlas points. Run maps adjacent to ones you've done.
2. **Yellow maps (T6–10).** Get **Voidstones** by beating the Eater of Worlds / Searing Exarch / Maven / Uber Elder questlines (Sirus too). Each Voidstone raises all map tiers.
3. **Red maps (T11–16).** Aim for 4 Voidstones so every map drops as T16.
4. **Pinnacle bosses** once the build is ready; uber versions after that. In 3.30, **Grim-Fated pinnacles** are the new top-end content.

Typical league-start timing: 4 Voidstones in a few days of play for a decent build; T17 / uber bosses later.

## Choosing a farming strategy

Pick **one or two mechanics** and specialise the atlas tree around them. Questions to ask:

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Does my build clear fast? | Speed strategies (Breach, Legion, Abyss, Harvest-style juicing) need clear speed |
| Does my build kill bosses? | Boss rushing, Fate-empowered bosses (3.30), Ultimatum need single target |
| Is it tanky? | Juiced maps and high Thread counts raise monster difficulty |
| Do I want low effort? | "Alch and go" + a simple mechanic (e.g. Essence, Ritual, Expedition) is the classic league-start strategy |

### League-start strategies that usually work
- **Essence + Harbinger/Breach early on**: low investment, steady currency.
- **Expedition**: logbooks and currency, needs a build that handles rares.
- **Ritual**: good for buying specific omens/items; slow-ish.
- **Legion / Abyss** (both reworked in 3.29): clear-speed builds.
- **Boss rushing / Fate stacking (3.30)**: good fit for strong single-target builds — likely a big league-start money maker because the league rewards are on bosses.
- **Delve / Heist / Sanctum**: side activities, marked on the atlas in 3.30.

## Money basics

- Sell in bulk on the **Currency Exchange** (in-game). Price-check items with Awakened PoE Trade.
- Early-league prices are high for everything: sell good drops on day 1–3, buy gear later when it gets cheaper.
- Check poe.ninja economy for what's valuable this league.
