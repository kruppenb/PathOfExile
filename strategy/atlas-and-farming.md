# Atlas and farming strategy

> **3.30 caveat:** the atlas tree is rebuilt from scratch and Scarabs are gone. Specific node paths from older guides are obsolete.
> The principles below still apply; fill in the "3.30 specifics" section once the patch notes and first atlas guides are out (Maxroll and poe-vault usually publish on launch day).

## Progression path (stable across leagues)

1. **Finish the campaign → white maps (T1–5).** Complete each new map once for atlas bonus and atlas points. Run maps adjacent to ones you've done.
2. **Yellow maps (T6–10).** Get **Voidstones** by beating the Eater of Worlds / Searing Exarch / Maven / Uber Elder questlines (Sirus too). Each Voidstone raises all map tiers.
3. **Red maps (T11–16).** Aim for 4 Voidstones so every map drops as T16.
4. **Pinnacle bosses** once the build is ready; uber versions after that. In 3.30, **Grim-Fated pinnacles** are the new top-end content.

Typical league-start timing: 4 Voidstones in a few days of play for a decent build; T17 / uber bosses later.

## Choosing a farming strategy

Pick **one or two mechanics** and specialise the atlas tree around them. Questions to ask:

| Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Does my build clear fast? | Speed strategies (Breach, Legion, Abyss, Harvest-style juicing) need clear speed |
| Does my build kill bosses? | Boss rushing, Fate-empowered bosses (3.30), Ultimatum need single target |
| Is it tanky? | Juiced maps and high Thread counts raise monster difficulty |
| Do I want low effort? | "Alch and go" + a simple mechanic (e.g. Essence, Ritual, Expedition) is the classic league-start strategy |

### League-start strategies that usually work
- **Essence + Harbinger/Breach early on**: low investment, steady currency.
- **Expedition**: logbooks and currency, needs a build that handles rares.
- **Ritual**: good for buying specific omens/items; slow-ish.
- **Legion / Abyss** (both reworked in 3.29): clear-speed builds.
- **Boss rushing / Fate stacking (3.30)**: good fit for strong single-target builds — likely a big league-start money maker because the league rewards are on bosses.
- **Delve / Heist / Sanctum**: side activities, marked on the atlas in 3.30.

## 3.30 atlas plan for Lightning Tendrils (Nov 16, day 3)

Sources: creator videos from Nov 13–15 in `videos/raw/` (Fubgun, Spicysushi, Aakoz, Pohx, POEGuy, Palsteron) and r/pathofexile via Arctic Shift. Profit numbers are each creator's own claim from day 1–2, unverified, and day-1 prices fall fast.

**What the league is finding strong**

| Strategy | Claim | Fit for Tendrils (strong boss damage, stand-still, slower clear) |
|---|---|---|
| **Shrines** (every shrine node; ~5 shrines a map, 2 buffs each, +90% effect) | "Strongest I've ever felt in a league start" (Spicysushi); "shrines completely carry" (Fubgun) | ✅ Take first. Free power and Acceleration shrines patch the slow clear. |
| **Exarch (red) altars** + magic pack size | ~5–6 div/hr day 1 (Fubgun), ~3–4 by day 2 (Spicysushi); fades by day 3–4, then switch to blue (Eater) altars | ✅ Any build can do it. Good while finishing the atlas. |
| **Blight**, 4 Threads | ~10 div/hr (Spicysushi): a Thread can turn one lane into rolling-currency monsters; blighted maps turn into Nightmare maps (50–60c each) | ✅ **Best fit.** You stand at the chokepoint, towers help, the currency lane is tanky (rewards single target). |
| **Harvest ("Sacred Flora")** + Mirage | 10–12 div/hr (Aakoz), mostly lifeforce; monsters are tough, skip the map boss | ✅ Good second pick: tanky mobs suit single-target builds. |
| **Legion** + shrines | "Exceptional" raw currency (Fubgun, on a geared bow) | ⚠️ Rewards fast clear, which is Tendrils' weak side. |
| **Expedition** | "Printing" for Pohx's RF | ❌ "You will die a lot … mobs are so rippy you cannot stand still to cast." |
| **Ultimatum** | POEGuy's favourite | ❌ He says stand-still builds lose to Ruin; a Reddit farmer died on round 13 of 14. |

The league mechanic's currency options seem to show up more with juicier maps (rarity / map-effect nodes), per Spicysushi (speculation).

## Money basics

- Sell in bulk on the **Currency Exchange** (in-game). Price-check items with Awakened PoE Trade.
- Early-league prices are high for everything: sell good drops on day 1–3, buy gear later when it gets cheaper.
- Check poe.ninja economy for what's valuable this league.
