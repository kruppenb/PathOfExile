# 3.30 endgame at a glance (Nov 21, 2026)

For Nick's Lightning Tendrils Elementalist (TehSenate, ~lvl 89, ~1M DPS). Trade league; Ruthless sections ignored.

## The ladder

1. **Finish the atlas + 4 Voidstones** → every map drops as T16.
2. **Farm** with the atlas tree (see [atlas-and-farming.md](atlas-and-farming.md): shrines/altars → Fated Nightmare boss rush).
3. **Pinnacles → Uber pinnacles → Grim-Fated pinnacles → Coven Queen** (the 3.30 final boss).

## The 4 Voidstones (current, since the 3.26–3.28 changes)

| Voidstone | Boss | How you open it | Nick |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eldritch | Searing Exarch **and** Eater of Worlds (one stone for both) | Black Star / Infinite Hunger, then influenced maps → Incandescent / Screaming Invitation | ✅ done |
| Originator ("Zana's") | Incarnation of Dread | Memory tears / Threads of Consciousness in red maps (Zana + Eagon story) end in a boss | ✅ probably (the "Zana" one) |
| Ceremonial | The Maven | Maven witnesses bosses → Crescent Splinters → 10 make **The Maven's Writ** | ⬜ |
| Decayed | Shaper **and** Elder (one source also allows Uber Elder) | 4 Shaper Guardian fragments → Shaper; 4 Elder Guardian fragments → Elder | ⬜ |

Sirus gives no Voidstone (one source). Old guides that say "Exarch, Eater, Maven, Uber Elder = one stone each" are out of date.

## Next steps, in order

1. **Shaper + Elder (Decayed).** Easiest pinnacles left; ~1M DPS is enough, and you can stand and channel through most of both fights. Run the guardian maps as they show up and save the fragments.
2. **Maven (Ceremonial).** Harder: the memory game and the "brain" phase are pure movement, so stop channelling and dodge. Let her witness guardians/map bosses to build the Writ. Aim for more DPS first (2–3M+ helps) so phases go fast.
3. Then you have 4 stones. Start the boss-rush farm.

## After 4 Voidstones

- **Uber pinnacles** (Uber Elder, Uber Shaper/Maven/Exarch/Eater/Sirus/Dread): rule of thumb 10M+ DPS and solid max-hit. Not yet on 1M; this is what the Fated boss farm pays for.
- **Grim Fates** drop from Fate-empowered bosses in high-tier maps. Invoke them on a **pinnacle** boss → Pinnacle Viscera (top Wicker Man tier) → the route to the **Coven Queen**. GGG cut Fate damage on Nov 17–18, but Fated bosses still "rip"; start with one Fate.
- **Nightmare maps** (blighted/fated maps turn into them; now also where meta-mod crafting recipes live) and **T17s**: endgame juice, later.
- 3.30 detail: Exarch/Eater/Maven quests give 2 atlas points each, Uber Elder 4 (150 total). Shaper/Elder/Conqueror fragments slotted into a map no longer get used up (and give no quantity).

## Keys / fragments to keep

- Shaper/Elder guardian fragments, Crescent Splinters / Maven's Writ: **keep** until you have the stone.
- After that, sell spare pinnacle fragments/invitations (they sell well early) or run them for drops.
- Grim Fates: hold or sell; check poe.ninja.

Sources: expcarry.com voidstone guide (Jul 2026, 3.28-era), timesaver.gg 3.29 guide (RMT site, marked), vtomes 3.30 Covens guide (Nov 18), poedb 3.30.0 patch notes, GGG 3.26.0 notes. Shaper+Elder vs Uber Elder for Decayed is from one source: check the stone's tooltip/atlas quest in game.

## How to fight Shaper, Elder and Maven (added Nov 21)

Sources: maxroll boss guides (Shaper/Elder Jun 2024, Maven Feb 2025), poedb, Mobalytics 3.28 endgame/atlas guides. Fights have not changed in years, but none of these pages is 3.30-dated.

### Shaper → Decayed (with Elder)
- **Unlock:** 4 Shaper Guardian maps (Hydra, Chimera, Phoenix, Minotaur; top-right quadrant) drop "occasionally from map bosses". Atlas node *Remnants of the Past* raises the chance. Kill each guardian → fragment. 4 fragments in the map device → Shaper's Realm: kill the 4 corner mini-bosses, centre portal → Shaper.
- **Phases:** 100→50%, intermission (The Uncreated), 50→25%, intermission (The Unshaped), 25→0%. Slow intermissions = Shaper heals.
- **Kills you:** *Beam* (15k elemental/sec): step sideways; he can't turn, so it's your burst window afterwards. *Teleport Slam*: walk out of the marker. *Bullet Hell* (phase 2+): run into **Zana's bubble**, then leave fast because a Beam usually follows. *Unshaped's Frostbolt volley*: dodge "at all costs". Phase 3 adds an invulnerable clone that copies one ability.
- **Tendrils:** channel freely between Beam/Slam, stop for Bullet Hell and the frostbolts.

### Elder → Decayed (with Shaper)
- **Unlock:** Elder Guardians (Eradicator, Constrictor, Purifier, Enslaver) replace the boss in **Elder-influenced T14+ maps**. Each guardian fight has 3 arena swaps (at ⅔ and ⅓ life). 4 fragments in the map device → The Elder.
- **Kills you:** *Ice Spears* aim at where you stand when he casts: move after. *Siphon*: leave the area at once. *Ring of Death*: stand **inside** the inner ring. *Tentacle Slam*: outrun it (Quicksilver). *Rising Slam*: keep moving while he spins. At 0% run into Zana's shield for the final shockwave.
- The "Shaper binds the Elder" restraint phase is (almost certainly) **Uber Elder only**.
- **Tendrils:** easiest of the three; mostly stand and channel, step for spears and siphon.

### Maven → Ceremonial
- **Unlock:** click the blue Maven button on the atlas. She watches map bosses; after enough witnessed maps you get an invitation fight with those bosses (counts rise: 3, 4, 5, 6 … 10 bosses). Guardian sets give *The Formed* (Shaper guardians) and *The Twisted* (Elder guardians). Invitations drop **Crescent Splinters**; 10 = **The Maven's Writ** → map device. Rolling an invitation to 65%+ quantity gives more splinters.
- **Phases:** 7, alternating Maven (1,3,5,7) and the **Nucleus/brain** (2,4,6).
- **Kills you:**
  - *Brain Blast* (Nucleus channel): run onto the **outer rotating platforms** before it fires.
  - *Beams*: touching one = Maven's Punishment, no life/ES recovery for 10 s (instant flasks still work). Walk in the two moving safe gaps; Flame Dash can blink through.
  - **Memory game** (phase 3 onward; 3 zones, later 6): she's invulnerable, so **stop casting**, watch the zones light up, stand in the first one early, then walk them in order. A wrong step makes it faster and slows you. If it goes wrong, logging out is a known fallback.
  - Phase 7: 5 beams with moving gaps + fire projectile (ignite). Drop Gravity Well puddles in the centre so they don't block you.
- **Tendrils:** the hard one for a stand-still build. Damage windows are short, so bring as much DPS as you can and treat it as a movement fight.
