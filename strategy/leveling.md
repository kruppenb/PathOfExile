# Leveling strategy (PoE 1, league start)

General principles that hold league to league. The 3.30 Fate system adds optional harder area bosses during the campaign:
fight them only if they're quick, the main goal of day 1 is to reach maps.

## Targets

| Milestone | Casual target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Act 5 (Kitava 1) | ~3–4 h | -30% resistances after Kitava 1 |
| Act 10 / Kitava 2 | ~8–12 h | Another -30% res. Need ~75% res capped soon after |
| First maps | Same session as Act 10 | White maps (T1–T5) to finish the atlas start |
| 4 ascendancy points (Normal + Cruel lab) | By Act 10 | Merciless lab ~level 70–75, Eternal/Uber lab later |

## Core rules

1. **Level with a strong generic skill, not necessarily your final one.** Common league-start levelers:
   Rolling Magma / Armageddon Brand / Holy Flame Totem / Freezing Pulse (casters), Galvanic Arrow / Caustic Arrow (bows),
   Sunder / Ground Slam / Smite / Perforate (melee), Raging Spirits / Absolution (minions). Swap to the main skill once it's online.
2. **Movement skill early:** Flame Dash / Leap Slam / Frostblink / Dash. Put a movement skill on a key and use it every pack.
3. **Prioritise movement speed boots, life, and resistances** over damage once you're out of Act 2. Gear check at each act boss.
4. **Vendor recipes for leveling:** Magic weapon + rustic sash + blacksmith's whetstone = +phys %; wand/sceptre + topaz + magic = +lightning;
   linked RGB items → Chromatic Orb. With socket colour restrictions gone (3.29), linking is easier — re-check how vendors now treat sockets.
5. **Buy key gems at Lilly Roth** after Act 6 (and Siosa in A3 library) so you're not waiting for drops.
6. **Do Labyrinth when overlevelled for it:** Normal lab ~level 33+, Cruel ~55+, Merciless ~68+.
7. **Flasks:** Quicksilver for speed, a Life flask, and pick up Bleed/Freeze/Ignite immunity flasks as you go.
8. **Don't full-clear.** Kill what's in the way, use waypoints, skip optional areas unless they hold a skill point / quest reward.

## Skill point quests (do not skip)

Act 1: The Dweller of the Deep, The Marooned Mariner (Allflame in Ship Graveyard), Bandits = **kill all three (Eramir) for 2 points** unless the build says Alira/Kraityn/Oak.
Act 2: The Way Forward (Deal with the Bandits counts too), Through Sacred Ground.
Act 3: Victario's Secrets, Piety's Pets.
Act 4: An Indomitable Spirit.
Act 5: In Service to Science, Kitava's Torments.
Act 6: The Puppet Mistress, The Cloven One, Fallen from Grace.
Act 7: Kishara's Star, Queen of Despair.
Act 8: Love is Dead, Reflection of Terror, The Gemling Legion.
Act 9: Queen of the Sands, The Ruler of Highgate.
Act 10: Vilenta's Vengeance, An End to Hunger.

(Exact quests occasionally change; check the leveling tracker in Exile UI.)
